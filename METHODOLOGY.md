# Evidence and result-reporting policy

## Reading the tracker

- **Reported work units** are counters local to each kind of computation. They are not interchangeable trial counts, unique inputs, frontier coverage, or solved equations.
- **Completed batches** indicate recorded work. An enabled instance with a completed batch has historical progress; it is not necessarily advancing at every instant.
- **Exact completions reported** describe retained local status. **Exact results passing both local checkers** means the stated mathematical conditions were recomputed separately. This is not external peer review.
- **Numerical candidates** are approximate experiments. They do not establish exact existence, stability, novelty, or a proof.
- **Health and timestamps** describe an observation, not guaranteed future availability. A missed or failed observation is not counted as successful research work.

The number of instances differs from the number of named mathematical questions. Several exponent configurations can concern one conjecture; several integers can be targets within one factorization project. A valid identity and a new scientific result are different claims.

## A report for a specific mathematical result

A future public report should include:

1. The precise question and a primary source, with the source's date or version.
2. The exact claim being made and its domain restrictions.
3. The explicit witness or mathematical object, when publication is authorized.
4. The mathematical verification, showing the relevant substitutions, equalities, inequalities, or divisibility steps for that particular witness.
5. A clear account of what those steps prove and what remains unproved.
6. A separate prior-work and novelty assessment, plus the status of any outside expert or journal review.

For example, a factor contribution is supported by exact integers with `N = d * q` and `1 < d < N`. That establishes a proper divisor of the stated integer. It does not by itself prove that either factor is prime, that the factorization is complete, that the divisor was previously unknown, or that a broader existence conjecture has been settled.

The public explanation will describe mathematical reasoning for the specific result. It will not publish the private software or its general implementation. Until a verified exact result is available and reviewed for publication, the tracker is progress evidence only.
