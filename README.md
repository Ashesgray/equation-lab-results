## What a future result report will contain

Each published result will focus on one specific mathematical problem: the question, its primary references, the exact claim, the relevant numbers or mathematical objects, and the mathematical steps that support that claim. It will distinguish the checked identity from any stronger conclusion and disclose whether novelty or external review has been established.

A valid contribution to one numerical target does not necessarily settle its broader conjecture. A finite search without a witness does not prove an infinite nonexistence claim. Project attribution is separate from formal scientific accreditation or prize eligibility.
